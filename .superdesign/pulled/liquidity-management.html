How Liquidity is Protected

Solvent calculates your Protected Liquidity = Reserve Requirement + Sum of Pending Obligations. This ensures you never deploy capital already promised away.

Deployable Capital = Total Assets − Protected Liquidity. The system prevents deploying beyond this safe limit, respecting all obligations first.

If coverage drops below 120% (amber) or 80% (red), alerts notify you. Use Recall to rebalance your strategy and maintain healthy coverage across all settlement periods.
