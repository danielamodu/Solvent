Frequently Asked Questions

What is obligation-aware treasury management?

It's a framework that prioritizes liabilities over assets. We track your future commitments so you only deploy capital that isn't already 'promised' elsewhere.

How does Solvent track cross-chain obligations?

We use a proprietary indexing layer that monitors multisigs and smart contracts across all supported EVM chains, unifying them into a single solvency view.

What are the security measures?

Solvent is non-custodial. We only monitor and suggest; we never hold your private keys. All strategies are audited by top-tier security firms.

Which chains are supported?

Currently we support Ethereum Mainnet, Arbitrum, Optimism, and Base. Polygon and Avalanche support is coming Q4 2024.

What are the fees?

Solvent charges a 0.5% annual management fee on deployed assets, plus a 10% performance fee on generated yield. Native DAO tracking is free.
